The Azure Velocity Engine is a completely custom, frame-perfect replay system. It stores EVERY input, X/Y velocity, releases, and gameplay states (like m_isholdingdash) inside the .azr format.

Azure Fixes & Checkpoints

Featuring next-gen Azure Fixes and Advanced Checkpoint Data. It tracks moving objects, gravity portals, and slope dashes, automatically redirecting inputs if you move or delete a checkpoint in practice mode.

Why choose Azure?

Because it's specifically built for Android64. No PC elitism, no paywalls, and no external FFmpeg dependencies. Just a beautiful, hand-coded Cocos UI with the most accurate velocity scanning ever seen on mobile.
